A two-sentence horror story, written as a program. `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes one original story to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly two sentences, under 400 characters, no trailing newline. Deterministic: the same story, byte for byte, on every run.

> I finally got my daughter to sleep by telling her the shadows on her wall were just the trees outside. Last night she pointed at the ceiling and whispered, "Then why is the tree inside with us?"

The twist is in the second sentence: the reassurance from the first sentence — *the shadows are just the trees outside* — collapses when the child points out the tree is inside with them. Nothing is explained; the reader fills in the worst of it.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
